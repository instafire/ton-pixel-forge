How it works

1 · Connect a wallet

Tap Select wallet (top right). Tonkeeper, Telegram Wallet, MyTonWallet and any TON Connect 2 wallet work. On desktop you can scan a QR with your phone wallet.

2 · Forge art

Every reroll generates a unique animated pixel piece from 5 traits — symbol, backdrop, palette, animation and effect. Lock traits you love, reroll the rest. Rarity is computed from trait weights: Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary.

3 · Mint (the cheap part)

Your first mint deploys a real TEP-62 NFT collection contract (one-time ~0.1 TON). Every NFT after that is a single message to that collection: ~0.055 TON all-in — gas plus a small storage reserve that the chain mostly refunds. No platform fee. Ever.

The animated GIF and its metadata JSON are hosted on this app's own Cloudflare Worker with KV storage (free tier, no API keys), so your NFT is fully viewable on getgems.io and explorers.

4 · Trade on getgems

Because the contracts are the exact standards getgems uses, minted NFTs appear on getgems.io, tonviewer and tonscan automatically. List them, sell them, transfer them — all standard.
